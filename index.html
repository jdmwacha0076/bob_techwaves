Frequently Asked

Answers to questions we hear often

If you don't find your answer here, send us a message.

What services does BOBTechWaves offer?

We build web systems, automation, event tech, QR solutions, MUALIKO invitations, SMS/WhatsApp tools, consulting, and cloud setup.

How much does a custom system cost?

Pricing depends on scope. After a free scoping call we send a clear quote with milestones.

How long does a project take?

Small projects ship in 2–4 weeks. Mid-sized systems take 6–10 weeks. National-scale platforms 3–6 months.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes post-launch support with bug fixes, adjustments, and training. Ongoing monthly agreements available after that.

Is my data secure with BOBTechWaves?

Yes. Encrypted connections, encrypted storage, role-based access, audit logs, and daily automated backups.

Can I see a demo before I commit?

Absolutely. Reach out via the contact form, WhatsApp, or phone and we'll arrange a live demo of any product.

Do you support both Swahili and English?

Yes — bilingual by design. Our systems, documentation, and training are available in both Swahili and English.

How do we start a project together?

Start with a free scoping call. We send a written proposal, then move through design, development, testing, launch, and support.
